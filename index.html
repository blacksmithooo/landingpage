Frequently asked questions

What’s new in Lumen (Gen 3)?

Gen 3 doubles battery life to around 8 hours, records in 3K and weighs less than Gen 2, in the same classic shape.

What can Lumen glasses do?

Take photos and video, play music, take calls, send messages and answer questions about what you see, all by voice or touch.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. The app and the built-in assistant are included with your glasses.

Can I make phone calls?

Yes. Pair them with your phone over Bluetooth to take and make calls hands-free.

How long does the battery last?

Around 8 hours of typical use, with up to 48 more hours from the charging case.

Do they work with prescription lenses?

Yes. Most frames support single-vision, progressive and transition lenses.

How much storage do they have?

32GB, enough for over 1,000 photos or about 100 short videos before you import them.

My glasses slip down. How do I fix that?

Visit an optician to adjust the temples, or try a frame in a narrower fit.
