Frequently asked questions

What’s new in Lumen (Gen 3)?

Gen 3 lasts up to 8 hours on a charge, records 3K video and has a lighter frame and a customisable action button.

What can Lumen glasses do?

Take photos and video, play music, make calls, send messages and answer questions about what you see, all by voice or touch.

Is there a subscription?

No. The app and assistant come with your glasses at no extra cost.

Can I make phone calls?

Yes. Pair with your phone over Bluetooth to make and take calls hands-free.

How long does the battery last?

Up to 8 hours of typical use, with up to 48 more hours from the charging case.

How is Gen 3 different from Gen 2?

Gen 3 roughly doubles battery life, adds 3K video and an action button, and weighs less.

Can I listen to music?

Yes. Stream from your favourite apps through the open-ear speakers.

Does every generation use the same case?

No. Each generation has its own charging case, shaped to fit its frames.

Where can I read about privacy?

See the privacy section of the Lumen app, or our privacy centre online.

How much storage is there?

32GB, enough for over 1,000 photos or about 100 short videos.

Which styles are available?

Wayline, Pilot and Cat-eye, each in several colours and lens options.

My glasses slip down. What can I do?

An optician can adjust the temples for you, or you can try a narrower fit.
